# Data provenance and reuse

Paper Trending is an independent research explorer, not an official conference
service. Its software license does not grant rights in third-party papers or
metadata. Source publications retain their original rights; no paper PDFs,
reviews, reviewer identities or private submissions are distributed.

Publication metadata comes from official conference event catalogs at
[ICLR](https://iclr.cc/), [ICML](https://icml.cc/) and [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/).
Individual title, author, abstract and source-link fields are retained for
scholarly discovery and attribution. Event catalogs are not necessarily identical
to final proceedings. The export records source corpus hashes and a snapshot date.

ICLR acceptance matching uses exhausted official **public** OpenReview submission
queries with audited decisions, including withdrawals and desk rejections. This
is not the organizer's total submission cohort. The public-pool baseline and
topic rate always share the same pool. Organizer-reported rates appear separately
as reference links, never as substitute denominators. Missing or invalid cohorts
remain unavailable; zero topic matches means 0/0, not a zero-percent rate.

[OpenReview's terms](https://openreview.net/legal/terms) define metadata to include
titles, authors and abstracts and apply a CC0 dedication to the extent the
submitter or OpenReview has a copyright interest in that metadata. Full works
have separate licenses. [PMLR's publication agreement](https://proceedings.mlr.press/pmlr-license-agreement.html)
provides its own attribution/license conditions. These terms are not a blanket
MIT license for conference data. Check the relevant record/source terms before
redistributing a modified dataset or using it for another purpose.

The browser export contains no original download audit paths or credentials.
Submission shards contain normalized searchable text and outcome labels, not
review bodies or author account profiles. Source/cohort references and original
audit hashes remain in the manifest. Raw authenticated downloads are not bundled.

To report incorrect metadata, attribution issues, or a removal request, open a
repository issue with the paper's public source URL. Never post credentials,
private submissions, or personal account details.
